# speechocean762 fixtures

Recordings and human scores in this folder are taken from the **speechocean762** corpus
(Junbo Zhang et al., 2021, https://www.openslr.org/101/), licensed under **CC BY 4.0**.
Audio samples were re-wrapped in a canonical 44-byte WAV header; the samples themselves are unchanged.
`manifest.json` lists the source utterance ids and the dataset's human scores.
